# Mobile room presence recovery

Saved-session restoration previously marked the client joined and rendered only its own participant before LiveKit connected. A failed initial join did not undo that optimistic state. Final SDK disconnect also called `handleLeave(false)`, which disconnected audio but left `isJoined`, participants and seats visible. The reconnect handler requested seat snapshots but did not rebuild the displayed membership from the SDK roster. Mobile suspension and slow data-channel startup could therefore leave a misleading solo room or stale participant list. These code paths are confirmed; the user's intermittent device-specific failure was not captured on a physical phone.

Joining now becomes true only after a successful LiveKit connection. Connecting/reconnecting has an explicit status banner. Failed joins and final disconnects clear joined state, participants and local seat claims without deleting the saved session. Generic websocket errors no longer claim a cloud quota failure without evidence.

`startRoomPresence` reads current SDK membership after connection, reconnection, foreground return, `pageshow` and `online`. Application snapshots are requested at 0, 1.5, 4 and 10 seconds to cover late data-channel readiness. A foreground-only 10-second roster poll heals missed participant events without recurring snapshot broadcasts. Teardown cancels timers/listeners and old rooms cannot overwrite current membership.

After the SDK's own reconnects have failed, a fresh token/rejoin is attempted at bounded delays (1.5, 4 and 8 seconds). Duplicate identity, participant removal and room deletion do not auto-rejoin. Failure after three attempts is shown on the entry screen. Explicit leave cancels scheduled recovery and aborts pending token requests; a late connection cannot silently re-enter after leaving.

Validation: `node scripts/check-room-presence.mjs` simulates slow channels, background/foreground changes, missed events, SDK reconnect roster changes, wake bursts, cleanup, successful/failed fresh-token recovery and cancellation during a pending attempt. Speaker-seat and memory-only post regressions pass. Type checking and production build validate page integration. The new helper passes ESLint; the existing page has 92 pre-existing lint errors (unchanged) and warnings decrease from 82 to 81, with no rule-count increases against HEAD. No actual users were joined, messaged or disconnected for testing.
