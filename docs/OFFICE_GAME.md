# Office arcade

Sit in a reserved office chair, then press G or tap the small G prompt under your character to open the floating arcade. A small mobile shortcut remains accessible when chat overlays the character. The world prompt renders above furniture paint. Standing, leaving the room or changing map unmounts the game. The panel is rendered above the room UI through a portal, has a draggable header clamped to the viewport, and fits narrow screens. A ResizeObserver also clamps it when tab contents change height. The game and ranking have separate tabs.

The first game is **30-second bug catching**: hit the highlighted cell by click, tap or number keys 1–9. Success starts at ten points and a streak adds up to twenty bonus points per hit. Wrong or repeated presses cost five points and reset the streak; missed targets reset it too. Targets change more quickly during the round and never occupy the same cell twice consecutively. Enter starts/retries; Escape closes. Keyboard events are captured only while focus is inside the arcade, preventing room movement, jump and chat shortcuts. Hidden tabs abort the current attempt, and closing or standing discards unfinished attempts.

Completed records contain a bounded seed and input trace. Each receiving client recomputes the score, checks the duration and trace bounds, and keeps one personal best per authenticated LiveKit identity. Ties use hits, misses and completion order. The room holds up to twenty personal bests in live memory; no browser or server persistence is added. Direct submissions must match the packet sender. A directed join snapshot supplies historical records; initial pull replies contain each participant's own record rather than twenty duplicate boards. Old records expire after six hours. This is a casual peer-shared leaderboard, not a server-authoritative competition or cheat-proof scoring service.

`node scripts/check-office-game.mjs` covers deterministic timing, combos, misses, duplicate hits, input validation, personal-best ranking and the real hook's room sharing, late joins and room isolation. `check-join-stability.mjs` includes the directed office ranking snapshot in its ten-participant regression.

Local mock-room browser QA verified reserved-seat G launch, Enter start, native clicks and number-key hits, a full 30-second 52-point result and personal rank. Space left the character in `up_sit` and focus stayed inside the game. Header dragging moved the window by 65×35 pixels. At 390×844 the 366×639-pixel window fit inside the screen, including after switching from the shorter ranking tab; game cells measured about 100 pixels. Escape, mobile tap launch and automatic close on standing were verified. This is Chromium viewport emulation, not a physical iPhone test. No live users or their voice channels were used for testing.
