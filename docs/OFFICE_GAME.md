# Office arcade: SKY BREAK

Sit in a reserved office chair and press G, or tap the nearby/mobile game prompt, to open the draggable floating window. Standing or changing map closes it. The portal sits above room panels, and its ResizeObserver clamps the window as tabs or viewport sizes change.

SKY BREAK replaces bug catching with a three-minute vertical aircraft shooter. Weapons fire automatically. Arrow keys/WASD move; dragging on the canvas provides pointer/touch movement. Space or the bomb button clears hostile bullets and damages enemies. P pauses. Three lives, short post-hit immunity, weapon upgrades, shields and replenishable bombs support survival. Enemy waves and projectiles grow harder over time; the first boss arrives at 25 seconds, with another scheduled every 35 seconds. Destroying bosses awards extra points. Surviving three minutes clears the mission and awards a bonus.

The pure seeded engine advances at a fixed 60 steps per second, independent of display frame rate, with bounded catch-up after a slow frame. Rendering uses a single 360×480 canvas and code-drawn planes, bullets, clouds and particles; no additional character atlases or large textures are loaded. Entity caps bound bullets, enemies, pickups and particles. Only the small game HUD updates React state, at most ten times per second. Closed, paused or hidden games do not keep a continuous animation loop. Hiding the page or opening the ranking tab pauses the game; finishing or closing stops it. Room keyboard shortcuts are suppressed while focus is in the arcade, so movement and bombs do not move or jump the seated avatar.

Versioned `flight-v1` records contain score, kills, bosses, damage and simulated survival duration. Bug-catching records are rejected and never mixed into the new ranking. Each room keeps up to twenty best records, one per LiveKit identity. Live submissions must match the authenticated packet sender; finite values, bounds, duration and terminal-state consistency are checked. Ranking is score, boss kills, total kills, survival time, damage and completion order. The shared board remains only in connected client memory, with directed join sync and small own-record pull replies; there is no browser or server persistence. This is casual peer-reported scoring, not a cheat-proof server-authoritative competition.

`node scripts/check-office-game.mjs` checks deterministic simulation, auto-fire and movement limits, damage/immunity, shields, powerups and bombs, boss spawning/destruction, full mission clear, entity caps, versioned record validation and the real ranking hook's authenticated submissions, late joins and room isolation.

Local mock-room browser QA verified seated G launch, Enter start, left-arrow flight, Space reducing bombs from two to one while the avatar remained `up_sit`, P pause, pointer drag steering at a 390×844 viewport, terminal game-over and a real 140-point result in first place. The 366×704-pixel floating window and approximately 328×438-pixel canvas fit the mobile viewport. The canvas artwork was exported and visually inspected. No live users or voice channels were used; mobile coverage is viewport emulation, not physical iPhone testing.
